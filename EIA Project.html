What is EIA?

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) is a process used to evaluate environmental effects of a project before decision-making. It considers environmental, social, and economic impacts. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

According to MoEF, EIA ensures sustainable use of natural resources and is mandatory for many large-scale projects in India. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

EIA Process (India)

  • Screening
  • Scoping
  • Impact Analysis
  • Public Consultation
  • Environmental Management Plan
  • Decision Making
  • Monitoring

EIA in India is governed under the Environmental Protection Act, 1986 and categorized into projects requiring central or state approval. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Project Report Summary

Objective: To analyze environmental impacts of development projects.

Methodology:

  • Data Collection (Air, Water, Soil)
  • Impact Prediction Models
  • Comparative Analysis

Findings:

  • Air pollution increases near industrial zones
  • Water contamination affects biodiversity
  • Noise pollution impacts human health

Conclusion:
